Legal & Policy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are important to us.

1. Information Collection: Step Up Dance Academy collects contact details (name, email, phone number) when you sign up for free trial classes, enroll in monthly programs, or submit contact enquiries.

2. Usage of Information: Your information is used strictly to communicate class schedules, trial confirmations, performance showcases, and studio updates. We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

3. Media Consent: Photography and videography captured during studio recitals, workshops, and rehearsals may be featured on our official website and social media channels with student/parent consent.

4. Data Protection: We employ administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access.

Academy Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please review our studio policies and code of conduct.

1. Registration & Tuition: All tuition fees are payable on a monthly or program basis prior to the 5th of each month. Trial sessions are limited to one free pass per prospective student.

2. Studio Code of Conduct: Students are expected to arrive 10 minutes prior to batch start time in appropriate dancewear and clean dance shoes. Respectful behavior toward instructors and fellow dancers is required at all times.

3. Attendance & Makeup Classes: Missed sessions due to personal absences can be made up within the same calendar month depending on batch availability and instructor approval.

4. Liability & Health Declaration: Students participate in physical dance activities at their own risk. Step Up Dance Academy is not liable for personal injuries or lost belongings within studio premises.
